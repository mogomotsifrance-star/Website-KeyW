The programme

Four tiers, one continuous journey

01

Preventative

Financial literacy training

BQA-accredited workshops on budgeting, responsible borrowing, saving, insurance and retirement planning. Delivered on site or virtually, in English or Setswana.

02

Corrective

Financial counselling

Confidential one-on-one sessions where a counsellor reviews income, debts and spending with the employee and agrees a practical plan.

03

Remedial

Debt rehabilitation

A structured programme for over-indebted employees: restructuring repayments, engaging creditors and rebuilding a sustainable monthly budget.

04

Supportive

Psychosocial counselling

Support for the emotional side of financial strain — anxiety, family conflict and the wellbeing issues that follow money problems.
